Things to Explore and know in rwanda

Rwanda is a beautiful country located in East Africa and is known as the “Land of a Thousand Hills” because of its many green hills and mountains. The country is famous for its cleanliness, safety, wildlife, and rapid development. Its capital city, Kigali, is one of the cleanest and most modern cities in Africa. Rwanda is well known for tourism attractions such as gorilla trekking in Volcanoes National Park, safaris in Akagera National Park, and beautiful views at Lake Kivu. The people of Rwanda are friendly and value peace, unity, and culture. Rwanda is also known for traditional foods, coffee, tea, and cultural dances that attract visitors from around the world.

    Kigali Convention Centre

    The Kigali Convention Centre (KCC) is a premier,53,000-square-meter facility in Rwanda's capital, known for its distinctive dome design inspired by traditional Rwandan homes.

    Nyandungu Eco Park

    Nyandungu Eco-Park is a 121-hectare restored wetland in Kigali, Rwanda, functioning as a premier urban eco-tourism destination

    Compaign against genocide

    The Campaign Against Genocide was a military operation (April–July 1994) led by the Rwandan Patriotic Army (RPA) to stop the 100-day genocide against the Tutsi.

    Volcanoes National Park

    Volcanoes National Park, located in northwestern Rwanda, is a premier African conservation area renowned as a primary habitat for endangered mountain gorillas and golden monkeys
